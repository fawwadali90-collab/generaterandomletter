What can you do with random generators?

Random tools are the unsung heroes of classrooms, game nights and workplaces. Teachers use the team generator to split classes into fair project groups, streamers spin the picker wheel live for giveaway winners, writers beat block with the random word generator, and D&D players roll the dice roller for ability checks and damage. Because every result uses cryptographic-quality randomness, nobody can argue the pick was rigged — the tool is the neutral referee.

From the simplest coin flip to full giveaway draws, each tool is built for one job and does it instantly: no accounts, no downloads, no waiting. Designers grab random colors for palette inspiration, parents find baby name ideas, and anyone facing a tough small choice can consult the yes/no oracle.

Frequently asked questions

Are these random generators really free?

Yes — all 12 tools are completely free with no signup, no limits and no watermarks.

Are the results truly random?

Yes. Our tools use the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues) where available, which provides cryptographic-quality randomness — far better than basic Math.random().

Do you store my lists or results?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your names, lists and picks never leave your device.

Can I use these tools in my classroom or stream?

Absolutely — teachers, streamers and event hosts use our tools every day. The picker wheel even has a fullscreen-friendly layout for projecting.

Which tool should I use for giveaways?

For live draws with up to 50 entries, the picker wheel is the most transparent choice. For longer lists and multi-winner draws, use the random list picker.

What's the best random tool for teachers?

The team generator for splitting classes, the picker wheel for calling on students, and the random letter generator for vocabulary games — all free and projector-friendly.

Can I roll dice for D&D here?

Yes — the dice roller handles d4 through d20, up to 6 dice per roll, with automatic totals. Perfect for ability checks and damage rolls.

Do the tools work on mobile?

Yes. Every tool is designed mobile-first and works in any modern browser on phones, tablets, and desktops.
