🎲 Dice Roller

Roll virtual dice — from a single d6 to six d20s — with instant totals.

About this tool

Left your dice bag at home? Roll up to six dice with any standard side count — d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 or d20. Each die is rolled independently with cryptographic randomness, and totals are added up for you automatically.

Popular uses

  • D&D & RPGs: ability checks, saving throws and damage rolls.
  • Board games: a digital stand-in for lost dice.
  • Teaching probability: roll often and graph the distribution.

Frequently asked questions

How do I roll dice online?

Choose how many dice and how many sides (d4 through d20), then press Roll Dice.

Can I roll a d20 for D&D?

Yes — select 1 die and 20 sides for a classic d20 roll, or roll multiple dice for damage.

Are the rolls fair?

Yes — every face of every die has an equal chance, using cryptographic-quality randomness.
