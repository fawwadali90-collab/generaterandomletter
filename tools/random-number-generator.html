About this tool

The classic random number generator: pick any minimum and maximum, choose how many numbers you need, and get instant results. Enable "no duplicates" for raffle draws and lottery-style picks, or sort the output for clean lists.

Popular uses

  • Raffles: assign ticket numbers and draw winners without duplicates.
  • Games: random encounters, loot rolls, bingo numbers.
  • Teaching: probability demos and math exercises.
  • Decisions: "pick a number between 1 and 10" — settled.

Frequently asked questions

How do I generate a random number between 1 and 100?

Set Min to 1, Max to 100, choose how many numbers you want, and press Generate.

Are the numbers truly random?

Yes — every number in your range has an exactly equal chance, using cryptographic-quality randomness.

What happens if I request more unique numbers than the range allows?

The tool automatically caps the count at the size of the range so you always get valid results.
