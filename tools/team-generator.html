About this tool

Enter any list of names and split them into balanced, random teams. The list is shuffled with cryptographic randomness and dealt round-robin, so teams are as even as possible and nobody can complain about stacked picks.

Popular uses

  • Classrooms: project groups, lab partners, debate teams.
  • Sports: pickup games, fantasy drafts, tournament brackets.
  • Work: breakout groups, secret santa pools, hackathon teams.
  • Parties: trivia teams, relay teams, game-night squads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I split names into random teams?

Paste your names (one per line), choose the number of teams, and press Generate Teams.

Are the teams really random?

Yes — names are shuffled with cryptographic-quality randomness before being dealt evenly into teams.

What if names don't divide evenly?

Teams are dealt round-robin, so the difference is never more than one person per team.
