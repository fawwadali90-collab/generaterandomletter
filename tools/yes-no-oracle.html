About this tool

The Yes/No Oracle is a digital magic 8-ball. Ask anything with a yes-or-no answer and receive a mystical response — from enthusiastic approval to cryptic deflection. Perfect for breaking decision deadlock with a laugh.

A note on wisdom

The oracle is entertainment, not advice. For pizza toppings, it's infallible. For career moves, maybe sleep on it too.
