About the picker wheel

Our picker wheel is a virtual spinning wheel — also called a wheel of names or spin the wheel picker — that selects a random entry from your list. Add names, choices, prizes or anything else — the wheel divides into colorful segments and spins with a realistic slowdown, clicking like a carnival wheel before landing on the winner with a fanfare and confetti celebration.

Because the draw happens visibly on screen, the wheel is the most trusted way to pick winners for giveaways on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, and Instagram Live. Paste your full entry list so the audience can verify everyone is included, then spin live — no one can claim the result was rigged.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste entries into the list box, one per line (up to 50).
  2. Click Update Wheel (or it updates as you type).
  3. Press SPIN and watch it land on your winner.
  4. Remove the winner and spin again for multi-round draws.

Popular uses

  • Giveaways & raffles: draw fair winners live on stream — the transparent choice for content creators.
  • Prize wheels: spin for discounts, prizes, and rewards at events.
  • Classrooms: pick students for questions, reading order, or classroom jobs.
  • Decisions: "what should we eat?" — let the wheel decide.
  • Party games: truth-or-dare prompts, challenge wheels, random dares.
  • Teams: spin to draft players or assign groups.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the picker wheel?

Type or paste your entries (one per line) into the list box, then press SPIN. The wheel animates and lands on a random winner.

Is the picker wheel really random?

Yes. The final stopping position is chosen with cryptographic-quality randomness, so every entry has an exactly equal chance of winning.

Can I use it for giveaways and raffles?

Absolutely — it's ideal for giveaways, classroom picks and raffles. For transparency, share your screen and spin it live.

How many entries can the wheel hold?

Up to 50 entries render smoothly on the wheel. For longer lists, use our random list picker instead.

Can I spin the wheel to pick a giveaway winner live?

Yes — share your screen on YouTube, Twitch, or Instagram Live and spin in front of your audience. Paste the full entry list first so viewers can verify everyone is included.

Does the wheel remove the winner after a spin?

Not automatically. To draw multiple winners, delete the winning line from the entry box and spin again — the wheel updates instantly.

What are some fun picker wheel ideas?

Prize wheels, truth-or-dare, dinner deciders, classroom student pickers, workout randomizers, and stream giveaways. Our blog lists 40 spin-the-wheel ideas.
