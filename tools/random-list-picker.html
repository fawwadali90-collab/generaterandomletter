About this tool

Drop in any list and draw any number of random winners. Winners are removed from the pool automatically, so you can keep drawing for 2nd and 3rd place without repeats. Need the whole list in random order instead? Hit shuffle.

Popular uses

  • Giveaways: draw winners from comment or entrant lists.
  • Raffles: fair, verifiable draws you can screen-share.
  • Secret Santa: shuffle the list and pair neighbors.
  • Prioritizing: shuffle a to-do list and tackle it top-down.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a random winner from a list?

Paste your entries one per line, choose how many winners to draw, and press Pick Winners.

Is the draw fair?

Yes — winners are selected with cryptographic-quality randomness; every entry has an equal chance.

Can I draw multiple winners without repeats?

Yes. Winners are automatically removed from the pool, so just press Pick Winners again for the next round.
