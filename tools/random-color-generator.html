About this random color generator

Stuck on a palette? Generate random colors instantly — each swatch shows its HEX, RGB and HSL values, and clicking a swatch copies the HEX code straight to your clipboard. Generate a single hero color or a full palette of up to 8.

Randomness is a legitimate design technique: constraints unlock creativity. Generate a palette and force yourself to design using only those colors — the unexpected combinations break the blank-canvas paralysis that hits every designer. Got something ugly? Even better — rescuing a "bad" random color into a working design is one of the fastest ways to grow your eye.

How to use it

  1. Choose how many colors (1–8).
  2. Hit Generate Colors.
  3. Click any swatch to copy its HEX, or Copy Palette to grab them all.
  4. Paste straight into CSS, Figma, Photoshop, or Canva.

Popular uses

  • Web design: theme inspiration and accent color ideas.
  • Branding: explore unexpected palette combinations for logos and identities.
  • Art challenges: limited-palette exercises for illustrators.
  • Development: placeholder colors for mockups and prototypes.
  • Decor & crafts: color schemes for rooms, weddings, and DIY projects.

Frequently asked questions

How do I copy a generated color?

Click any color swatch to copy its HEX code instantly, or use Copy Palette to grab all of them.

What is a random color generator used for?

Design inspiration, branding palettes, website themes, art projects, and breaking out of color ruts.

What color formats are shown?

Every color displays HEX, RGB and HSL values — ready for CSS, Figma, Photoshop, or any design tool.

How do designers use random colors?

As creative constraints: generate a palette and force yourself to design with only those colors. Random limits break creative blocks and spark unexpected combinations.

Can I generate a whole color palette at once?

Yes — generate up to 8 colors per click and copy the full palette as HEX codes in one go.

How do I check if a color combination looks good?

Trust your eye first, then check contrast ratios for text readability. Tweaking a random find — shifting hue or saturation — often turns an accident into a palette.

Is the color selection truly random?

Yes. Each RGB channel is drawn with cryptographic-quality randomness, so all 16.7 million colors have an equal chance.
