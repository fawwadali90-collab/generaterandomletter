About this random name generator

Stuck naming a character — or a baby? Our name generator combines popular real first and last names into fresh, natural-sounding full names. Because every combination uses genuine names rather than gibberish syllables, results feel believable — perfect for fiction, games, and mockups.

Choose first + last for full identities, first only for gamertags and character call-signs, or last only for surnames. Generate up to 20 at a time and keep clicking until one jumps out at you.
