About this tool

No coin in your pocket? Our virtual coin flip gives you a perfectly fair 50/50 result with a satisfying flip animation. Every toss is tracked so you can see your heads-vs-tails score build up.

Popular uses

  • Settling debates: who picks the movie, who does the dishes.
  • Sports: virtual coin toss for kickoff or first serve.
  • Games: binary random outcomes without a die.
  • Probability lessons: flip 100 times and watch it converge on 50/50.

Frequently asked questions

Is an online coin flip really 50/50?

Yes. It uses cryptographic-quality randomness — exactly 50% heads, 50% tails, every flip.

Can I track multiple flips?

Yes — running heads/tails totals appear under the coin, and you can reset anytime.
