About this tool

Weak passwords get cracked in seconds; strong ones take centuries. This generator creates passwords using your browser's cryptographic random number generator — the same quality of randomness used in security software. Important: everything happens on your device. We never see, store or transmit your passwords.

Tips for strong passwords

  • Use at least 16 characters — length beats complexity.
  • Never reuse passwords across sites; use a password manager.
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever it's offered.

Frequently asked questions

Is this password generator safe?

Yes. Passwords are generated locally in your browser with cryptographic randomness and never leave your device.

How long should my password be?

At least 16 characters with mixed character types. Longer is always stronger.

Do you store my passwords?

No — nothing is stored, logged or transmitted. Once you leave the page, it's gone.
