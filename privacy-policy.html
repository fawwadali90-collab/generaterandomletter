Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Short version: our tools run entirely in your browser. The names, lists, and picks you enter never leave your device — we don't see, store, or transmit them.

1. Information we collect

Tool inputs: none. Everything you type into our generators (names, lists, entries) is processed locally on your device by JavaScript running in your browser. It is never sent to our servers.

Automatically collected data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. We use this only for security and to understand aggregate usage; we do not use it to identify you personally.

Contact: if you contact us, we receive whatever information you choose to share (such as your email address) and use it only to respond.

2. Cookies

We use a minimal number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Essential/functional: your browser may store small preferences locally (such as sound on/off) so the site works as expected.
  • Advertising cookies: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though some site features may not work as intended.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

We may display ads served by Google AdSense and other advertising networks. These third parties may collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you. They have their own privacy policies; we encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies page.

4. Children's privacy

Our tools are family-friendly and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data security and retention

Because tool inputs never leave your device, there is nothing for us to retain or breach on that front. Any information you send us directly (e.g., via contact) is kept only as long as needed to respond, and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you. Since we hold essentially no personal information from tool usage, most requests will simply confirm that. To make a request, contact us using the details on our contact page.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision, and continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via our contact page.
